(54) 发明名称
三酮类除草剂环磺草酮的合成工艺
    > EN: Title of invention: Synthesis process for the triketone-class herbicide tembotrione (环磺草酮)
(57) 摘要
本发明涉及一种三酮类除草剂环磺草酮的合成工艺,包括有以下步骤:1)2-氯-6-甲磺酰基甲苯的合成;2)2-氯-3-乙酰基-6-甲磺酰基甲苯的合成;3)2-氯-3-甲基-4-甲磺酰基苯甲酸的合成;4)2-氯-3-甲基-4-甲磺酰基苯甲酸甲酯的合成;5)2-氯-3-溴甲基-4-甲磺酰基苯甲酸甲酯的合成;6)2-氯-3-(2,2,2-三氟乙氧基)甲基-4-甲磺酰基苯甲酸的合成;7)2-氯-3-(2,2,2-三氟乙氧基)甲基-4-甲磺酰基苯甲酸3-氧代-1-环己烯酯的合成;8)环磺草酮的合成。本发明的有益效果在于:缩短了反应步骤和时间,提高了中间体的收率;反应条件更温和,更安全;成本降低。
    > EN: Abstract. The present invention relates to a synthesis process for the triketone-class herbicide tembotrione (环磺草酮), comprising the following steps: 1) synthesis of 2-chloro-6-(methanesulfonyl)toluene; 2) synthesis of 2-chloro-3-acetyl-6-(methanesulfonyl)toluene; 3) synthesis of 2-chloro-3-methyl-4-(methanesulfonyl)benzoic acid; 4) synthesis of methyl 2-chloro-3-methyl-4-(methanesulfonyl)benzoate; 5) synthesis of methyl 2-chloro-3-(bromomethyl)-4-(methanesulfonyl)benzoate; 6) synthesis of 2-chloro-3-[(2,2,2-trifluoroethoxy)methyl]-4-(methanesulfonyl)benzoic acid; 7) synthesis of 3-oxo-1-cyclohexen-1-yl 2-chloro-3-[(2,2,2-trifluoroethoxy)methyl]-4-(methanesulfonyl)benzoate; 8) synthesis of tembotrione. The beneficial effects of the present invention are that: the reaction steps and time are shortened, and the yield of the intermediates is raised; the reaction conditions are milder and safer; the cost is lowered.

1. 1.三酮类除草剂环磺草酮的合成工艺，包括有以下步骤：
    > EN: 1. A process for the synthesis of the triketone herbicide 环磺草酮 (tembotrione), comprising the following steps:
1) 1)2-氯-6-甲磺酰基甲苯的合成
将2-氯甲苯、催化剂无水三氯化铝和溶剂加入到反应瓶中，在0℃-15℃间滴加甲磺酰氯，滴加完毕，升至室温继续反应，LC跟踪至反应完全，将反应物倒入冰水中，用溶剂萃取，浓缩，得2-氯-6-甲磺酰基甲苯；
    > EN: 1) Synthesis of 2-chloro-6-methylsulfonyltoluene
2-Chlorotoluene, the catalyst anhydrous aluminium trichloride and solvent are added to a reaction flask; methanesulfonyl chloride is added dropwise at between 0℃ and -15℃ [printed as 0℃-15℃, see notes]; after the dropwise addition is complete the mixture is warmed to room temperature and the reaction continued; LC is used to follow the reaction to completion; the reaction mixture is poured into ice water, extracted with solvent and concentrated to give 2-chloro-6-methylsulfonyltoluene;
2) 2)2-氯-3-乙酰基-6-甲磺酰基甲苯的合成
在反应容器中加入2-氯-6-甲磺酰基甲苯，无水三氯化铝和溶剂，在15-20℃温度条件下，滴加乙酰氯，滴加完毕后，在室温继续反应1-5h，反应完毕，将反应物倒入冰水和盐酸的混合物中，分出有机层，水层用溶剂萃取，合并有机层，水洗，浓缩得2-氯-3-乙酰基-6-甲磺酰基甲苯；
    > EN: 2) Synthesis of 2-chloro-3-acetyl-6-methylsulfonyltoluene
2-Chloro-6-methylsulfonyltoluene, anhydrous aluminium trichloride and solvent are added to a reaction vessel; at a temperature of 15-20℃ acetyl chloride is added dropwise; after the dropwise addition is complete the reaction is continued at room temperature for 1-5h; when the reaction is complete the reaction mixture is poured into a mixture of ice water and hydrochloric acid; the organic layer is separated, the aqueous layer is extracted with solvent, the organic layers are combined, washed with water and concentrated to give 2-chloro-3-acetyl-6-methylsulfonyltoluene;
3) 3)2-氯-3-甲基-4-甲磺酰基苯甲酸的合成
在装有搅拌器的反应容器中分别加入步骤2)得到的2-氯-3-乙酰基-6-甲磺酰基甲苯和次氯酸钠水溶液，加热反应，用稀盐酸酸化，过滤，水洗，干燥得2-氯-3-甲基-4-甲磺酰基苯甲酸；
    > EN: 3) Synthesis of 2-chloro-3-methyl-4-methylsulfonylbenzoic acid
The 2-chloro-3-acetyl-6-methylsulfonyltoluene obtained in step 2) and aqueous sodium hypochlorite solution are added separately to a reaction vessel fitted with a stirrer; the mixture is heated to react, acidified with dilute hydrochloric acid, filtered, washed with water and dried to give 2-chloro-3-methyl-4-methylsulfonylbenzoic acid;
4) 4)2-氯-3-甲基-4-甲磺酰基苯甲酸甲酯的合成
将步骤3)得到的2-氯-3-甲基-4-甲磺酰基苯甲酸和甲醇加入带有回流冷凝管的反应瓶中，加入催化剂浓硫酸或对甲苯磺酸，回流反应1-10h，蒸除过量甲醇，得2-氯-3-甲基-4-甲磺酰基苯甲酸甲酯；
    > EN: 4) Synthesis of methyl 2-chloro-3-methyl-4-methylsulfonylbenzoate
The 2-chloro-3-methyl-4-methylsulfonylbenzoic acid obtained in step 3) and methanol are added to a reaction flask fitted with a reflux condenser; the catalyst concentrated sulfuric acid or p-toluenesulfonic acid is added; the mixture is refluxed for 1-10h; excess methanol is distilled off to give methyl 2-chloro-3-methyl-4-methylsulfonylbenzoate;
5) 5)2-氯-3-溴甲基-4-甲磺酰基苯甲酸甲酯的合成
将2-氯-3-甲基-4-甲磺酰基苯甲酸甲酯溶于溶剂中，加入适量的过氧苯甲酸，在回流的条件下，滴加溴素，滴完后继续反应1-10h，冷却，水洗，浓缩得2-氯-3-溴甲基-4-甲磺酰基苯甲酸甲酯；
    > EN: 5) Synthesis of methyl 2-chloro-3-bromomethyl-4-methylsulfonylbenzoate
Methyl 2-chloro-3-methyl-4-methylsulfonylbenzoate is dissolved in solvent; an appropriate amount of peroxybenzoic acid is added; under reflux conditions bromine is added dropwise; after the addition is finished the reaction is continued for 1-10h; the mixture is cooled, washed with water and concentrated to give methyl 2-chloro-3-bromomethyl-4-methylsulfonylbenzoate;
6) 6)2-氯-3-(2,2,2-三氟乙氧基)甲基-4-甲磺酰基苯甲酸的合成
将2-氯-3-溴甲基-4-甲磺酰基苯甲酸甲酯溶于溶剂中，加入2,2,2-三氟乙醇钠，在室温下搅拌，然后水洗，浓缩得2-氯-3-(2,2,2-三氟乙氧基)甲基-4-甲磺酰基苯甲酸乙酯，将残余物溶于溶剂中，加入氢氧化钠水溶液，回流反应1-4h，冷却，加入稀盐酸酸化，溶剂萃取，水洗干燥浓缩得2-氯-3-(2,2,2-三氟乙氧基)甲基-4-甲磺酰基苯甲酸；
    > EN: 6) Synthesis of 2-chloro-3-(2,2,2-trifluoroethoxy)methyl-4-methylsulfonylbenzoic acid
Methyl 2-chloro-3-bromomethyl-4-methylsulfonylbenzoate is dissolved in solvent; sodium 2,2,2-trifluoroethoxide is added; the mixture is stirred at room temperature, then washed with water and concentrated to give ethyl 2-chloro-3-(2,2,2-trifluoroethoxy)methyl-4-methylsulfonylbenzoate; the residue is dissolved in solvent, aqueous sodium hydroxide solution is added, the mixture is refluxed for 1-4h, cooled, acidified by adding dilute hydrochloric acid, extracted with solvent, washed with water, dried and concentrated to give 2-chloro-3-(2,2,2-trifluoroethoxy)methyl-4-methylsulfonylbenzoic acid;
7) 7)2-氯-3-(2,2,2-三氟乙氧基)甲基-4-甲磺酰基苯甲酸3-氧代-1-环己烯酯的合成
在反应瓶中加入2-氯-3-(2,2,2-三氟乙氧基)甲基-4-甲磺酰基苯甲酸、N，N-二甲基甲酰胺和溶剂，在室温下，滴加氯化亚砜，升温回流搅拌，蒸除溶剂，残余物加入二氯甲烷， 冷却后，加入环己二酮和碱的混合物，室温搅拌，反应物水洗，干燥，浓缩得油状物2-氯-3-(2,2,2-三氟乙氧基)甲基-4-甲磺酰基苯甲酸3-氧代-1-环己烯酯；
    > EN: 7) Synthesis of 3-oxo-1-cyclohexenyl 2-chloro-3-(2,2,2-trifluoroethoxy)methyl-4-methylsulfonylbenzoate
2-Chloro-3-(2,2,2-trifluoroethoxy)methyl-4-methylsulfonylbenzoic acid, N,N-dimethylformamide and solvent are added to a reaction flask; at room temperature thionyl chloride is added dropwise; the temperature is raised and the mixture is stirred at reflux; the solvent is distilled off; dichloromethane is added to the residue; after cooling, a mixture of cyclohexanedione and base is added; the mixture is stirred at room temperature; the reaction mixture is washed with water, dried and concentrated to give the oil 3-oxo-1-cyclohexenyl 2-chloro-3-(2,2,2-trifluoroethoxy)methyl-4-methylsulfonylbenzoate;
8) 8)环磺草酮的合成
在装有干燥管的反应瓶中加入2-氯-3-(2,2,2-三氟乙氧基)甲基-4-甲磺酰基苯甲酸3-氧代-1-环己烯酯、氰基丙酮和三乙胺溶于溶剂中在室温持续搅拌，蒸除溶剂，萃取，水洗，干燥，浓缩得米黄色固体环磺草酮。
    > EN: 8) Synthesis of 环磺草酮 (tembotrione)
3-Oxo-1-cyclohexenyl 2-chloro-3-(2,2,2-trifluoroethoxy)methyl-4-methylsulfonylbenzoate, cyanoacetone and triethylamine are added to a reaction flask fitted with a drying tube, dissolved in solvent and stirred continuously at room temperature; the solvent is distilled off, and the mixture is extracted, washed with water, dried and concentrated to give 环磺草酮 (tembotrione) as a beige solid.

None 背景技术
    > EN: Background art
[0002] 除草剂环磺草酮(Tembotrione)，化学名称：2-(2-氯4-甲磺酰基-3-[(2,2,2-三氟乙氧基)甲基]苯甲酰基)环己烷-1,3-二酮。由拜耳公司2007年研制的三酮类玉米田除草剂，其活性高于目前市场上的硝环磺酮和甲基磺草酮，对作物安全。对多种杂草有很强的杀灭作用，无残留活性，有较强的抗雨水冲刷能力，且除草谱广，能有效防除辣子草属、旋花属、婆婆纳属、猪殃殃等杂草。
    > EN: The herbicide tembotrione (Tembotrione), chemical name: 2-(2-chloro 4-methylsulfonyl-3-[(2,2,2-trifluoroethoxy)methyl]benzoyl)cyclohexane-1,3-dione. A triketone maize-field herbicide developed by Bayer in 2007; its activity is higher than that of 硝环磺酮 and 甲基磺草酮 currently on the market, and it is safe to the crop. It has a very strong killing action on many kinds of weeds, has no residual activity, has fairly strong resistance to being washed off by rain, and has a broad weed-control spectrum, being able to effectively control weeds such as Galinsoga (辣子草属), Convolvulus (旋花属), Veronica (婆婆纳属) and cleavers (猪殃殃).
[0003] 现有技术采用的合成路线有以下缺点：(1)采用甲硫醇钠气味比较大，废水中的硫醇难于处理，造成环境污染；(2)生产成本过高；(3)采用N-溴琥珀酸亚胺作溴化剂，该溴化剂昂贵。
    > EN: The synthesis routes used in the prior art have the following drawbacks: (1) the use of sodium methanethiolate gives off a comparatively strong odour, and the mercaptan in the waste water is hard to treat, causing environmental pollution; (2) the production cost is too high; (3) N-bromosuccinimide is used as the brominating agent, and this brominating agent is expensive.
