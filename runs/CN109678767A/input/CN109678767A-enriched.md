(54) 发明名称
一种除草剂环磺酮的合成工艺
    > EN: Title of invention: A synthesis process for the herbicide tembotrione (环磺酮)
(57) 摘要
本发明公开一种除草剂环磺酮的合成工艺，包括：1)先加入2-氯-3-甲基-4-甲磺酰基苯甲酸甲酯、溶剂、催化剂、氢溴酸，然后滴加双氧水，反应后水洗、浓缩、重结晶，得到溴代物2-氯-3-溴甲基-4-甲磺酰基苯甲酸甲酯；2)将溴代物、碱1、催化剂、溶剂、2,2,2-三氟乙醇进行反应，反应后过滤，水洗，浓缩；加入碱2、水，反应后酸化，过滤，洗涤，烘干得醚化物2-氯-3-(2,2,2-三氟乙氧基)甲基-4-甲磺酰基苯甲酸；3)将醚化物、催化剂、氯化亚砜、溶剂反应后脱尽溶剂；加入1,3-环己二酮、溶剂，滴加三乙胺；反应后加入丙酮氰醇，水洗，分层，油层脱尽溶剂，加溶剂进行重结晶，过滤，烘干得米黄色固体环磺酮。本发明提高中间体的收率，更环保、更安全、降低生产成本。
    > EN: Abstract. The present invention discloses a synthesis process for the herbicide tembotrione (环磺酮), comprising: 1) first charging methyl 2-chloro-3-methyl-4-methylsulfonylbenzoate, a solvent, a catalyst and hydrobromic acid, then adding hydrogen peroxide dropwise; after the reaction, washing with water, concentrating and recrystallising to give the brominated compound methyl 2-chloro-3-bromomethyl-4-methylsulfonylbenzoate; 2) reacting the brominated compound, base 1, a catalyst, a solvent and 2,2,2-trifluoroethanol; after the reaction, filtering, washing with water and concentrating; adding base 2 and water, and after the reaction acidifying, filtering, washing and drying to give the etherified compound 2-chloro-3-(2,2,2-trifluoroethoxy)methyl-4-methylsulfonylbenzoic acid; 3) reacting the etherified compound, a catalyst, thionyl chloride and a solvent, then stripping off the solvent completely; adding 1,3-cyclohexanedione and a solvent, and adding triethylamine dropwise; after the reaction adding acetone cyanohydrin, washing with water, separating the layers, stripping the solvent completely from the oil layer, adding solvent to recrystallise, filtering and drying to give tembotrione as a rice-yellow (beige) solid. The present invention raises the yield of the intermediates, and is more environmentally friendly, safer and lower in production cost.

None 背景技术：
    > EN: Background art:
[0002] 除草剂环磺酮(Tembotrione)，化学名称：2-{2-氯-4-甲磺酰基-3-[(2,2,2-三氟乙氧基)甲基]苯甲酰基}环己烷-1,3-二酮，是由拜耳公司2007年研制的三酮类玉米田除草剂，其活性高于硝磺酮(硝磺草酮、甲基磺草酮)，对作物安全。环磺酮主要用于玉米田，是芽后HPPD(对羟基苯基丙酮酸双氧化酶)抑制剂类除草剂，可阻断植株体内异戊二烯基醌的生物合成，引起失绿、褪色、组织坏死，最终在2周内死亡。环磺酮除草谱广、除草适期长，主要靶标玉米田芽后中晚期各种阔叶杂草与禾本科杂草，对蓟、田旋花、婆婆纳、辣子草、鼬瓣花和猪殃殃等防效优异，且对后茬作物无药害，是美国玉米田最重要的除草剂之一，每季最大用药量为100g/hm2，主要剂型为：可分散油悬浮剂。
    > EN: The herbicide tembotrione (Tembotrione), chemical name: 2-{2-chloro-4-methylsulfonyl-3-[(2,2,2-trifluoroethoxy)methyl]benzoyl}cyclohexane-1,3-dione, is a triketone-class maize-field herbicide developed by Bayer in 2007; its activity is higher than that of nitrosulfone/mesotrione (硝磺酮) (mesotrione, methyl sulcotrione), and it is safe to the crop. Tembotrione is used mainly in maize fields; it is a post-emergence HPPD (4-hydroxyphenylpyruvate dioxygenase) inhibitor herbicide, which can block the biosynthesis of prenyl (isoprenoid) quinones inside the plant, causing chlorosis, discoloration and tissue necrosis, and finally death within 2 weeks. Tembotrione has a broad weed-control spectrum and a long window of application; its main targets are the various broadleaf weeds and grass weeds of the mid-to-late post-emergence stage in maize fields, and it has excellent efficacy against thistle, field bindweed, speedwell, gallant soldier, hemp-nettle and cleavers, causes no phytotoxicity to the succeeding crop, and is one of the most important herbicides in United States maize fields, with a maximum application rate of 100 g/hm2 per season; the main formulation type is: oil dispersion (dispersible oil suspension concentrate).
[0003] 目前开发的三酮结构HPPD类除草剂还有硝磺草酮、磺草酮、双环磺草酮等，环磺酮的活性要高于硝磺草酮，相较于其他品种，环磺酮对多种杂草有很强的杀灭作用，无残留活性，有较强的抗雨水冲刷能力，且除草谱更广。
    > EN: The triketone-structure HPPD herbicides developed so far also include mesotrione, sulcotrione, benzobicyclon and others; the activity of tembotrione is higher than that of mesotrione, and compared with the other varieties tembotrione has a very strong killing action on many kinds of weeds, has no residual activity, has a comparatively strong resistance to rain wash-off, and its weed-control spectrum is broader.
[0004] 2007年，环磺酮在奥地利首先取得登记，产品中加入了安全剂双苯噁唑酸(isoxadifen)，商品名为Laudis，用于防除玉米田禾本科杂草和阔叶杂草；同年，该产品还在美国和匈牙利取得登记；2008年在巴西登记；2009年在葡萄牙登记。2010年，环磺酮与噻酮磺隆(thiencarbazone)的复配产品Capreno在美国上市，其后在欧洲和拉美取得进一步登记。
    > EN: In 2007 tembotrione was first registered in Austria; the safener isoxadifen was added to the product, whose trade name is Laudis, for the control of grass weeds and broadleaf weeds in maize fields. In the same year the product was also registered in the United States and Hungary; in 2008 it was registered in Brazil; in 2009 it was registered in Portugal. In 2010 Capreno, the co-formulated product of tembotrione with thiencarbazone, was launched in the United States, and subsequently obtained further registrations in Europe and Latin America.

    > EN: Heilongjiang University's patent No. CN105601548A reports a process for synthesising tembotrione from 3-chloro-2-methylaniline as the starting material in which, when preparing methyl 2-chloro-3-bromomethyl-4-methylsulfonylbenzoate, expensive NBS is likewise used as the brominating agent, a large amount of solid waste is produced, and the yield is on the low side, only 75.2%.

    > EN: Wuhan Institute of Technology's patent No. CN104292137A reports a process for synthesising tembotrione from 2-chlorotoluene as the starting material; when preparing methyl 2-chloro-3-bromomethyl-4-methylsulfonylbenzoate, the comparatively hazardous elemental bromine is used as the brominating agent and the yield is on the low side, only 70%; when preparing 2-chloro-3-(2,2,2-trifluoroethoxy)methyl-4-methylsulfonylbenzoic acid, the sodium 2,2,2-trifluoroethoxide used cannot at present be produced on a large scale and the raw material cannot be bought; when preparing tembotrione, the cyanoacetone used is too expensive so production costs are high, and moreover the intermediate 3-oxo-1-cyclohexen-1-yl 2-chloro-3-(2,2,2-trifluoroethoxy)methyl-4-methylsulfonylbenzoate is isolated and worked up separately, which makes the process cumbersome, and the yield is on the low side, only 69%.
