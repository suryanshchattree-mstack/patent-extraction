  36 |     > EN: Title of invention: A process for the preparation of 2-chloro-3-(alkoxymethyl)-4-(methanesulfonyl)benzoic acid
  37 | (57) 摘要
  38 | 本发明涉及一种2-氯-3-烷氧基甲基-4-甲磺酰基苯甲酸的制备方法，所述方法包括以下步骤：原料2-氯-3-溴甲基-4-甲磺酰基苯甲酸甲酯在碱性物质存在下于三级醇溶剂中发生酯解反应得到2-氯-3-溴甲基-4-甲磺酰基苯甲酸盐；2-氯-3-溴甲基-4-甲磺酰基苯甲酸盐在碱性物质存在下与醇反应或者将2-氯-3-溴甲基-4-甲磺酰基苯甲酸盐与碱金属醇盐反应，得到所述2-氯-3-烷氧基甲基-4-甲磺酰基苯甲酸。本发明提供的制备方法以三级醇为溶剂，原料在碱性物质存在下发生酯解反应，降低了杂质的含量，目标化合物的收率和品质得到了大幅的提升，为最终合成高品质环磺酮和呋喃磺草酮原药提供了保障。
  39 |     > EN: Abstract. The present invention relates to a process for the preparation of 2-chloro-3-(alkoxymethyl)-4-(methanesulfonyl)benzoic acid, the process comprising the following steps: the starting material methyl 2-chloro-3-(bromomethyl)-4-(methanesulfonyl)benzoate undergoes an ester cleavage reaction in a tertiary alcohol solvent in the presence of a basic substance to give a 2-chloro-3-(bromomethyl)-4-(methanesulfonyl)benzoate salt; the 2-chloro-3-(bromomethyl)-4-(methanesulfonyl)benzoate salt is reacted with an alcohol in the presence of a basic substance, or alternatively the 2-chloro-3-(bromomethyl)-4-(methanesulfonyl)benzoate salt is reacted with an alkali metal alkoxide, to give the said 2-chloro-3-(alkoxymethyl)-4-(methanesulfonyl)benzoic acid. In the preparation process provided by the present invention a tertiary alcohol is used as the solvent and the starting material undergoes the ester cleavage reaction in the presence of a basic substance, which lowers the content of impurities; the yield and the quality of the target compound are greatly raised, which provides a safeguard for the final synthesis of high quality tembotrione and tefuryltrione technical material.
